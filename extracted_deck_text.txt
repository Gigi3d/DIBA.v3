Tokenization Extends the Bitcoin Promise.
Bitcoin established a proven framework for peer-to-peer value transfer without reliance on
centralized intermediaries. Tokenization builds upon this architecture. By utilizing this infrastructure,
markets can migrate the instruments that underpin the global economy — bonds, equities, credit,
and real assets — to a more efficient settlement rail.
The access problem is structural. Approximately 80–82% of Americans have no meaningful
exposure to institutional-grade financial instruments. Globally, this exclusion affects the vast majority
of the population. This is not driven by investor preference, but by the operational limitations of
legacy capital markets.

EVM Infrastructure Has a Structural Flaw
Institutional Capital Cannot Ignore.
After Bitcoin, Ethereum introduced programmable electronic value. The experiment yielded a large
ecosystem. It also generated a body of evidence indicating that the design philosophy of placing
contract logic in a shared, public execution environment accessible to every participant carries risks
that scale with the size of the system.
Re-entrancy attacks, oracle manipulation, maximal extractable value, sandwich attacks, and bridge
exploits are not isolated vulnerabilities awaiting patches. They are predictable consequences of the
architecture. The empirical data reflects this clearly.

Client-Validated Contracts on Bitcoin.
RGB represents a decade of development aimed at creating private, scalable smart contract
protocols that utilize Bitcoin for settlement without requiring consensus changes or compromising
network efficiency.
"Peter Todd's single-use seal primitives cryptographically anchor every token and asset state to a
Bitcoin UTXO. Validation is private. Settlement is on Bitcoin."
Source: RGB Protocol (originating from single-use seal commitments)
Each state transition is validated client-side by the recipient, rather than by miners, validators, or a
network of nodes maintaining a shared ledger. The blockchain records only a cryptographic
commitment. The asset logic can systematically handle complex contractual demands: waterfall
distributions, maturity triggers, investor gating, and transfer restrictions, with all execution occurring
off-chain.

WHAT BITCOIN SETTLEMENT ENABLES: SELECTED USE CASES
USE CASE 01 · STABLECOINS
Dollar-Denominated
Settlement on Bitcoin
Tether has begun issuing USDT natively on Bitcoin via RGB, becoming the
first major stablecoin to leverage Bitcoin rails. This is not a peg or a
wrapped token; it is a native issuance anchored to a Bitcoin UTXO, settled
with Bitcoin finality, and validated client-side. Institutions can hold, transfer,
and settle USD-equivalent value without EVM bridge risk.
USE CASE 02 · PRIVATE
CREDIT
Loan Facilities with
Programmable Logic
RGB supports complex contract schemas including waterfall distributions,
seniority tranches, maturity triggers, and default provisions. All logic is
validated off-chain by the participating parties. This architecture removes
trustees and reconciliation lag, with settlement finality anchored to the
Bitcoin block. Private credit funds can manage instruments without
custodial intermediaries.
USE CASE 03 · SECURITIES
Equities with Compliant
Transfer Restrictions
RGB21 schemas support automated KYC/AML gating, accredited investor
verification, jurisdictional limits, and lock-up periods encoded directly into
the contract rather than enforced by post-trade policy. Bonds can execute
coupon automation and enforce transfer restrictions cryptographically, with
ownership recorded on Bitcoin.
USE CASE 04 · FUND UNITS
LP Interests with Atomic
Subscription
LP interests and co-investment vehicles can be issued as RGB tokens.
Subscription and redemption processes execute atomically: the investor
delivers payment and the fund delivers the LP token synchronously within a
single transaction. Secondary trades operate identically, eliminating T+2
settlement lag and counterparty credit risk.
USE CASE 05 · SOVEREIGN
National Bond Registries
Governments exploring digital bond issuance can anchor registries to
Bitcoin, utilizing a ledger with an unbroken 15-year security record. The
sovereign does not rely on third-party intermediaries. The contract state is
held by the bondholder while Bitcoin provides absolute timestamping and
settlement guarantees.

How Institutions Should Think About
Bitcoin-Native Infrastructure.
The institutional risk assessment for Bitcoin-settled capital markets differs fundamentally from EVM-
based DeFi. The architectural vulnerabilities are distinct, the empirical track record is fundamentally
different, and the regulatory trajectory provides superior clarity.
Technology Risk
MITIGATED
RGB has been in development since 2016 and is supported by multiple independent audits. Bitcoin's
base layer carries a 15-year zero-exploit record. Contract logic is validated entirely client-side,
eliminating the shared public execution environments that expose EVM chains to systemic attack.
Counterparty Risk
STRUCTURALLY ELIMINATED
Atomic swap settlement means no settlement lag, no escrow counterparty, and no custodian holding
assets between execution and delivery. T+0, both legs, or no trade. That is the entire counterparty
risk conversation.
Regulatory Risk
REDUCING
The GENIUS Act (U.S., 2025) created the first federal stablecoin framework, while MiCA in Europe
provides a clear path for tokenised asset issuance. Furthermore, Bitcoin is explicitly recognized as a
commodity by U.S. regulators, representing the clearest regulatory classification of any digital asset.
Liquidity Risk
MANAGED
Lightning Network provides 24/7 liquidity for asset transfers. Bitcoin's $1.3 trillion market cap is the
deepest liquidity pool of any digital asset. Secondary trading via DIBA Marketplace with atomic
settlement removes execution risk.
Custody Risk
SELF-SOVEREIGN
BitMask Wallet is non-custodial. Private keys never leave the client device. This is not a policy
position. It is the architecture.
